How it works

One hand-written loop. Every risky action is gated, every claim is checked.

Plan

A single LLM call turns your sentence into a short, editable plan.

Act

The agent picks exactly one validated browser tool per turn and executes it in Python.

Approve

Financial or destructive clicks are blocked until you approve them here.

Verify

A deterministic, no-LLM check compares the saved record against ground truth.
